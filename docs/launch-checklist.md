# Mini-Course Launch Checklist

Walk this top to bottom the week you ship. It assumes the seven chapters
are done. Check boxes for real; unchecked boxes are the reason launches
slip.

## Promise and outline (chapters 1–2)

- [ ] Promise sentence is filled: "After this lesson, you will be able to ____, on your own, in about ____ minutes."
- [ ] Both blanks are checkable — a stranger could verify you kept the promise.
- [ ] Outline table exists: `Step | Minutes | Visible result`.
- [ ] Total taught time is 60–90 minutes or less; no single sitting exceeds ~25 watch + ~10 do.
- [ ] Every step's result is something the reader can see, hear, or run.
- [ ] Each ending of a sitting names the restart command.

## Recording (chapter 3)

- [ ] Room check passed: quiet, echo-free, window in front not behind.
- [ ] One video per outline step, named to match (`01-hook.mp4`, …).
- [ ] Promise spoken in the first 30 seconds; finish line in the last 30.
- [ ] Every video plays at 1× on a phone and a laptop before upload.
- [ ] Hook video has human-written captions.

## Pages and printables (chapter 4)

- [ ] Free lesson page uses the template's full skeleton.
- [ ] Free page contains at least one *complete* teachable step (not a trailer).
- [ ] Checkpoints after every video ("You should now see ____, if not check ____").
- [ ] Printable drafted, **printed on paper, checked in grayscale**, exported as PDF.
- [ ] Printable lives next to the step it serves, named after the promise.
- [ ] "What is not included" paragraph is honest and specific.
- [ ] Everything works on a phone: pages, video, PDF, buy link.

## Venue and payment (chapter 5)

- [ ] Real current rates collected for your two finalist venues.
- [ ] Fee math rerun at your real price and a conservative sales count.
- [ ] Break-even computed for any venue with a monthly fee.
- [ ] Venue chosen in pen; checkout tested with a real (refunded) card.
- [ ] Files: course content lives in one folder you can back up in a single command.

## Refunds (chapter 6)

- [ ] Five facts published: window (14 days default), reason optional, full refund, access stops, one email address.
- [ ] Policy text sits near the buy button, not only in a footer.
- [ ] Approval reply saved as a snippet.
- [ ] One real refund walkthrough done in your dashboard so it is boring on the day.

## Newsletter drips (chapter 7)

- [ ] Plain newsletter list exists with an honest signup promise (cadence stated).
- [ ] Welcome email sends automatically at signup.
- [ ] Launch Email 1 written and scheduled for launch day.
- [ ] Launch Email 2 (day 3) and Email 3 (day 7) drafted with one link each.
- [ ] Buyer drips B and C drafted (restart command; finish-line check).
- [ ] Next weekly "write the plain email" 30 minutes is on your calendar.

## Launch day, the boring half

- [ ] Buy link earns its click: test it in a private window, logged out.
- [ ] Access delivery works end-to-end: buy → receive → open → play → print.
- [ ] Refund window start date is stated on the receipt/order email.
- [ ] Support email on the page goes to an inbox a human answers within two days.
- [ ] Backup: zip of the course folder stored somewhere other than the only disk.

## After launch (the half everyone skips)

- [ ] Day 3: check open/reply counts; change nothing yet. Watch, don't tinker.
- [ ] Day 7: send the finish-line check; read every reply like it is the product.
- [ ] Day 14: first completions? Ask two finishers one question: "What nearly stopped you?" Answer shapes v1.1.
- [ ] First refund: honor gladly, save the reason, never argue.
- [ ] 30 days: revisit the fee math with real numbers. Write the real ones next to the guesses.
